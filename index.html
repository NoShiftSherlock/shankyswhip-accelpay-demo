Meet Shanky

A legendary Irish jockey

Shanky was a legendary Irish jockey (and part-time distiller). Banned from racing horses, he took up riding ostriches.

Shanky's Whip is named in his honor: a one-of-a-kind spirit from County Cavan, Ireland.

How this works

This is Shanky's Whip's own Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed. The cart and checkout are Shopify. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer in the buyer's state, or about 30 minute delivery through Gopuff where it is stocked.
